Foundation

Infrastructure should serve
the whole education community.

Paper & Slate is an independent open-foundation initiative creating shared standards and tools for developers, educators, institutions, researchers, and public data organizations.

Our mission

Make educational systems easier to connect, understand, and improve.

Education depends on documents, data, websites, software, identifiers, and public information that rarely share a common structure. Paper & Slate develops neutral specifications and practical tools that let those systems communicate without placing any institution behind a vendor boundary.

What we are building

A family of independently versioned standards covering educational documents, organization discovery, structured school information, courses, curriculum standards, provenance, and implementation tooling.

What success looks like

  • A school can publish accurate information once and make it discoverable everywhere.
  • A developer can adopt a standard without permission, fees, or a commercial relationship.
  • An educator can move structured content between tools without losing meaning.
  • Public data can retain source, confidence, and update history.

Principles

How we decide
what belongs.

○

Open by default

Specifications, source, discussions, and decisions should be public.

◇

Implementation neutral

Define behavior and meaning without prescribing one vendor stack.

↔

Interoperable

Prefer composable standards with explicit extension points.

⌁

Privacy respecting

Do not normalize publishing personal or sensitive information by default.

◎

Education first

Optimize for real educational workflows rather than abstract elegance.

△

Evidence driven

Use examples, research, testing, and implementation feedback.

＋

Extensible

Allow deliberate namespaces without weakening the common core.

✓

Durable

Version carefully and preserve compatibility wherever practical.
