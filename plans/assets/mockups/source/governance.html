Decision model

Transparent now.
Transferable later.

Routine changes are handled by project maintainers. Material changes use public RFCs, documented review periods, and recorded decisions.

Founder stewarded.
Maintainer reviewed.
Publicly discussed.

1

Proposal

An issue or RFC establishes the problem, scope, alternatives, and compatibility impact.

2

Review

Named maintainers and implementers test the proposal against real use cases.

3

Decision

The decision, rationale, dissent, and follow-up work are published permanently.
