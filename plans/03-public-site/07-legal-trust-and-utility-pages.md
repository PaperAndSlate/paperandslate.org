# Legal, Trust, and Utility Pages

## Required pages

- Privacy
- Terms of Use
- Accessibility
- Security
- Trademark Policy
- License Overview
- Code of Conduct
- Contact
- Status link
- 404 and error pages

Legal text should receive appropriate legal review before being represented as final.

## Privacy page

Document:

- analytics provider and events collected;
- server logs;
- newsletter data sent to Kit;
- cookies and local storage;
- retention periods;
- contact for access/deletion requests;
- no sale of personal data;
- international processing if relevant.

The theme preference may use local storage. Search may be client-side or send queries to Typesense. Explain each.

## Terms

At minimum:

- informational nature of draft specifications;
- no warranty;
- separate open-source and documentation licenses;
- acceptable site use;
- external links;
- trademark restrictions;
- jurisdiction after legal review.

## Accessibility statement

Include:

- target standard: WCAG 2.2 AA;
- known limitations;
- testing methods;
- contact route;
- response commitment;
- date last reviewed.

## Security page

Publish:

- responsible disclosure process;
- security contact;
- whether encrypted email is available;
- expected response timing;
- scope;
- safe-harbor language after review;
- link to repository `SECURITY.md`.

Do not route vulnerabilities through a public issue tracker.

## Trademark policy

Cover:

- official versus community implementations;
- compatibility statements;
- logo usage;
- project forks;
- events and education;
- prohibited endorsement claims.

## License overview

Explain the license matrix in plain language and link to full license texts.

## 404 page

Provide:

- plain explanation;
- search;
- Projects, Documentation, Governance, and News links;
- optional report-broken-link action.

Avoid jokes that undermine a serious technical reference site.

## Error page

State whether cached or static content remains available. Include retry and issue-reporting actions.

## Maintenance state

The editorial site should remain available even if search or newsletter dependencies are down. Degrade those components independently.
