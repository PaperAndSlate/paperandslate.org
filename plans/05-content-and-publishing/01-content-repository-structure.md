# Content Repository Structure

## Recommended structure

```text
content/
├── foundation/
│   ├── about.mdx
│   ├── mission.mdx
│   ├── principles.mdx
│   ├── funding.mdx
│   ├── contact.mdx
│   └── reports/
├── governance/
│   ├── charter.md
│   ├── governance.md
│   ├── maintainers.md
│   ├── contributing.md
│   ├── code-of-conduct.md
│   ├── security.md
│   ├── conflicts.md
│   ├── trademarks.md
│   ├── licenses.md
│   ├── rfcs/
│   └── decisions/
├── projects/
│   ├── file-system.mdx
│   ├── discovery.mdx
│   └── ...
├── news/
│   └── 2026/
├── docs/
│   ├── getting-started/
│   ├── concepts/
│   ├── guides/
│   └── governance/
├── people/
│   └── people.yml
├── roadmap/
│   └── roadmap.yml
└── redirects.yml
```

Project-specific technical documentation imported from the standards monorepo is generated under `.generated` and must not be copied into this tree.

## Content schemas

Use a central schema package:

```text
packages/content/src/schemas/
├── foundation.ts
├── project.ts
├── docs.ts
├── news.ts
├── rfc.ts
├── decision.ts
├── person.ts
├── roadmap.ts
└── report.ts
```

Every collection has:

- Zod schema;
- TypeScript type;
- fixture examples;
- validation error formatting;
- test coverage;
- migration notes when fields change.

## Asset ownership

### Public content assets

Keep optimized source images under:

```text
content-assets/[collection]/[slug]/
```

Build scripts copy or transform them into a predictable public asset path.

### Imported docs assets

Keep beside the source docs. The ingestion pipeline fingerprints and copies them into generated assets.

### Brand assets

Keep in:

```text
packages/design-system/assets/
```

Do not duplicate the logo in individual content directories.

## Drafts

Frontmatter:

```yaml
status: draft
```

Drafts:

- appear in local development;
- appear in authenticated preview environments if preview protection exists;
- are excluded from production pages, feeds, search, and sitemap;
- may still be built in CI for validation.

## Scheduled content

Use `publishedAt` and `status: scheduled`. Production excludes future content. A scheduled workflow rebuilds after the timestamp.

## Content IDs

Use stable explicit IDs for projects, people, RFCs, and decisions. News posts can use their slug as ID after publication, but changing a published slug requires a redirect.

## Generated content rule

Generated content folders carry a header or manifest saying:

> Generated by `docs:ingest`. Do not edit directly.

CI should fail if generated files are committed accidentally unless the repository intentionally snapshots them for fixtures.
